# Ten Jokes

![A smiling robot holding a sign that says "404: Joke not found"](foo.svg)

1. Why did the calendar get promoted? It had a lot of dates lined up.
2. My pencil started a band. It draws a crowd.
3. Why was the computer cold? It left its Windows open.
4. I asked the elevator for advice. It said things have their ups and downs.
5. Why did the tomato blush? It saw the salad dressing.
6. My lamp told a joke. It really brightened the room.
7. Why don't mountains get tired? They peak early.
8. The baker got a raise because they always rose to the occasion.
9. Why was the math book stressed? It had too many problems.
10. I tried to catch some fog, but I mist.
