# 🦖 Dinosaurs

Dinosaurs are a diverse group of reptiles that first appeared during the Triassic period, around 230 million years ago. They dominated terrestrial ecosystems for over 160 million years until the Cretaceous–Paleogene extinction event about 66 million years ago.

## Famous Dinosaurs

| Name | Period | Diet | Fun Fact |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| Tyrannosaurus rex | Late Cretaceous | Carnivore | Had one of the strongest bites of any land animal. |
| Triceratops | Late Cretaceous | Herbivore | Its frill may have been used for display. |
| Stegosaurus | Late Jurassic | Herbivore | Its brain was roughly the size of a walnut. |
| Velociraptor | Late Cretaceous | Carnivore | It was about the size of a turkey and had feathers. |
| Brachiosaurus | Late Jurassic | Herbivore | Its front legs were longer than its hind legs. |

## Did You Know?

- Birds are living descendants of theropod dinosaurs.
- The word "dinosaur" means "terrible lizard" and was coined by Sir Richard Owen in 1842.
- Fossils of dinosaurs have been found on every continent, including Antarctica.
